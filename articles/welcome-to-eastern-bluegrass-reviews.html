Welcome to Eastern Bluegrass Reviews

Honest automotive and powersports coverage from the heart of Kentucky.

Eastern Bluegrass Reviews was created to give buyers straightforward information without the sales pitch.

Our coverage includes cars, trucks, SUVs, motorcycles, ATVs, UTVs, tractors, trailers, boats, RVs, and other equipment. We publish news, first looks, dealership walkarounds, comparisons, ownership updates, and full reviews.

What makes our coverage different?

We focus on the questions real buyers ask: Is it comfortable? Is it practical? Does it feel well built? Can it handle the job? Is the price justified?

Our standard: Everything that moves, honestly reviewed.

What readers can expect

Our reviews consider performance, comfort, technology, utility, quality, practicality, value, and an overall recommendation.
